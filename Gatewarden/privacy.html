Gatewarden privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Gatewarden is a game by Mauro Dreissig. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it on Android or in a web browser.

Information we collect

None. Gatewarden does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no purchases, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Information kept on your device

The game keeps your saved games and your settings (sound volume, music, weather, and the furthest wave you reached) in its own storage on your device. They never leave it, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Internet

The game plays offline and makes no connection to the internet. Installing and updating it through Google Play is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Gatewarden is not directed at children under 13, and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
